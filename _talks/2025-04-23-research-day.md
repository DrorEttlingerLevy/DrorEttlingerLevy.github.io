I presented my PhD research at the **Faculty Research and Explorer Day at the Technion**, held on April 23, 2025, at the Rabin Building.

The event brought together PhD and Master's students from across the faculty to present their research in a poster competition, with an emphasis on communicating the main scientific contribution of each project to researchers from different disciplines.

I presented my work on using **underwater acoustics, signal processing, and machine learning to monitor fish behavior in aquaculture**. The research explores how sounds produced by fish can provide information about feeding activity and how AI-based analysis can transform these acoustic signals into useful information for precision aquaculture.

The broader goal is to develop non-invasive monitoring tools that can support better feeding decisions, reduce feed waste, and improve the environmental sustainability of intensive aquaculture.
